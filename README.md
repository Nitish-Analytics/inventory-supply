# Inventory & Operations Dashboard — Excel

## 📊 Project Overview

An interactive **Inventory & Operations Dashboard** built in Microsoft Excel to analyze inventory health, supplier performance, stockouts, order fulfillment, and regional operational performance.

The project simulates an e-commerce/supply-chain operations environment and demonstrates how raw operational data can be transformed into actionable business insights using Excel.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The dashboard was designed to answer key operational questions:

- How healthy is the current inventory?
- How much capital is tied up in overstock?
- Which products are experiencing stockout problems?
- Which suppliers have the highest delivery delays?
- Which supplier-product combinations are underperforming?
- Which regions have inventory or fulfillment issues?
- How many orders are cancelled, pending, or returned?
- Which products have unusually high cancellation rates?
- Where should Operations teams prioritize their attention?

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 1. Overstock Capital

₹16.30 Cr of inventory value is tied up in overstock, representing **56.36% of total inventory value**.

### 2. Stockout Risk

Low Stock inventory averages **4.94 stockout days** and accounts for approximately **50.6% of total stockout days**.

### 3. Supplier Performance

**TechSource → Bluetooth Speaker** is the most concerning supplier-product combination, with an **83.33% late-delivery rate** (10 out of 12 deliveries were late).

Other high-risk combinations include:

- GlobalMart → Sneakers: 64.29%
- UrbanSupply → Hoodie: 53.85%
- PrimeGoods → Cricket Bat: 53.33%
- GlobalMart → Coffee Maker: 50.00%

### 4. Regional Performance

**South** has the lowest fulfillment rate at **64.44%** and the highest average stockout days at **0.97**.

**East**, meanwhile, has the highest overstock exposure, with **₹4.81 Cr** tied up in overstock inventory.

### 5. Order Performance

Overall order performance:

| Metric | Result |
|---|---:|
| Fulfillment Rate | 67.20% |
| Pending Rate | 12.80% |
| Cancellation Rate | 11.87% |
| Return Rate | 8.13% |

### 6. Product-Level Risks

- **Storage Box** has the highest cancellation rate at **24.29%**.
- **Coffee Maker** has a **20.25%** cancellation rate.
- **Sneakers** have the highest pending rate at **20.48%**.
- **Bedsheet Set** has the highest return rate among the analyzed products at **12.70%**.
- **Cricket Bat** has the highest average stockout days at **1.25 days**.

---

## 💡 Operational Takeaways

The analysis indicates that the business does not have a simple "too much inventory" or "too little inventory" problem.

Instead, there appears to be an **inventory allocation and supply-chain efficiency problem**:

- Significant capital is tied up in overstock.
- Certain products experience recurring stockouts.
- Some supplier-product combinations have severe delivery delays.
- Regional fulfillment performance varies considerably.
- Certain products have elevated cancellation and pending-order rates.

These findings can help Operations teams prioritize:

1. Supplier performance reviews
2. Inventory reallocation
3. Replenishment planning
4. Overstock reduction
5. Regional investigation
6. Product-level fulfillment improvements
